Privacy policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Shoco respects your privacy. This policy explains, in general terms, how we handle information when you visit getshoco.com or use a Shoco product.

Information we collect

If you join the Shoco launch list, we collect the email address you provide so we can send occasional product and launch updates. We may also collect basic technical information needed to keep the site secure and working, such as browser type, device information, and approximate usage data.

How we use information

We use your launch-list email to communicate about Shoco and use technical information to maintain security and improve the site. We do not sell personal information or use the launch list for unrelated marketing.

Sharing and retention

We may use trusted service providers to operate our products and send updates. They may access information only as needed to provide those services. We keep launch-list information until it is no longer needed or until you ask us to remove it.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about, update, or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the signup form on the Shoco homepage.
